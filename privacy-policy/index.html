omoshuwa_podcast Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 8, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how omoshuwa_podcast handles information in connection with our podcast content distribution services.

2. Information We Collect

Our automated content distribution tools do not collect any personal information from users, viewers, or listeners. Specifically:

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or contact information through our distribution tools.
  • We do not use cookies or tracking technologies on this website.
  • We do not access or store any data from social media platform users.

3. TikTok Integration

Our TikTok integration uses the Content Posting API exclusively to publish our own video content. The integration:

  • Only accesses the app owner's TikTok account (no third-party user data).
  • Uses OAuth tokens (access_token and refresh_token) stored locally on the app owner's machine.
  • Uses video.upload and video.publish scopes to upload short video clips.
  • Uses video.list scope only to verify successful upload status.
  • Uses user.info.basic and user.info.profile scopes to verify account identity.
  • Does not share any data with third parties.

4. Other Platform Integrations

We also distribute content to YouTube, Instagram, X (Twitter), and Threads. Similar to TikTok, these integrations only access the app owner's accounts and do not collect any third-party user data.

5. Data Retention

OAuth authentication tokens are stored locally on the app owner's machine and are automatically refreshed as needed. No user data is retained in any database or cloud storage.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our contact form.
